See a customer's previous orders, refunds, cancellations and refused deliveries on the WooCommerce order screen, before you ship.

== Description ==

Customer Lens shows who you are about to ship to. On every WooCommerce order it lists the customer's previous orders and counts what went wrong before: refunds, cancellations, failed payments and refused cash-on-delivery parcels.

Orders are matched by email, phone and address, so guest orders and customers who change their email still add up to one history.

Every signal is a number or a date. There is no score, and the plugin never cancels an order on its own.

This plugin is in development. The full description, screenshots and FAQ come with version 1.0.
